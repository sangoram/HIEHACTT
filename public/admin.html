Center team codes

Each center’s whole team shares one code, so everyone at that center sees the same patients and nobody sees another center’s. Codes need at least 6 characters. Setting a new code signs everyone at that center out; turning a center off blocks its sign-in.

All data

Every patient record entered at any center — active ones, and ones a team has removed from its list (kept for you here). The JSON file holds every field; the CSV has one summary row per patient.
